About Sadat Academy

Sadat Academy for Management Sciences – Assiut Branch is a leading educational institution dedicated to providing high-quality academic programs and professional training. It focuses on preparing students with the knowledge, skills, and experiences they need to succeed in their careers and contribute positively to society.

Our student organization, Infinity Team, is affiliated with Sadat Academy and works to complement its academic mission by offering educational, cultural, and recreational activities. We aim to support students in discovering their talents, gaining practical experience, and developing both academically and personally during their university journey.

Who We Are

Our Vision

Our Committees

Discover the heart of Infinity Team's operations and development

HR Committee

Main Responsibilities:
  • Recruiting and selecting new members.
  • Organizing internal meetings and follow-ups.
  • Evaluating members’ performance and team development.
  • Maintaining team engagement and motivation.
What You Will Gain:
  • Leadership and people management skills.
  • Communication and conflict resolution experience.
  • Experience in recruitment and evaluation processes.
  • Organizational and administrative skills.

PR Committee

Main Responsibilities:
  • Managing external relations and partnerships.
  • Communicating with sponsors and guest speakers.
  • Representing the organization in official events.
  • Handling formal communication and networking.
What You Will Gain:
  • Professional communication skills.
  • Negotiation and networking experience.
  • Confidence in dealing with external entities.
  • Event representation experience.

Scientific & Cultural (S&C)

Main Responsibilities:
  • Organizing academic workshops and seminars.
  • Preparing educational content and training sessions.
  • Managing cultural events and awareness campaigns.
  • Supporting research and knowledge-sharing initiatives.
What You Will Gain:
  • Public speaking and presentation skills.
  • Research and content preparation experience.
  • Critical thinking and analytical abilities.
  • Academic and cultural development.

Marketing Committee

Main Responsibilities:
  • Creating marketing strategies for events and campaigns.
  • Managing social media content and promotions.
  • Designing promotional plans to increase engagement.
  • Analyzing campaign performance and audience reach.
What You Will Gain:
  • Digital marketing skills.
  • Content creation and branding experience.
  • Strategic thinking and campaign planning abilities.
  • Real-world marketing analytics exposure.

Organization & Events Committee (OR)

Main Responsibilities:
  • Planning and organizing events, workshops, and trips.
  • Coordinating between different committees for smooth execution.
  • Managing logistics, schedules, and resources for all activities.
  • Ensuring events run on time and meet quality standards.
What You Will Gain:
  • Event planning and project management skills.
  • Team coordination and leadership experience.
  • Problem-solving under real-life conditions.
  • Organizational and operational experience applicable in professional settings.

Our Achievements

Highlights of our journey and success

April 2025 | Educational & Cultural Achievement

Cairo Cultural Trip

An organized educational visit to Cairo including the Pyramids of Giza, the Citadel of Salah El-Din, and Khan El Khalili. The initiative aimed to enhance cultural awareness, strengthen historical understanding, and support experiential learning beyond the classroom environment, reflecting the organization’s commitment to structured developmental activities.

May 2025 | Organizational Development Achievement

Annual Closing Ceremony & Member Recognition

The organization held its Annual Closing Ceremony to conclude the year’s activities in a structured and professional setting. The event included the recognition and honoring of outstanding members and contributors in appreciation of their commitment, performance, and impact throughout the year. This initiative reflects the organization’s dedication to fostering a culture of appreciation, motivation, and institutional excellence.

October 2025 | Leadership & National Engagement Achievement

Participation in SUSTAINABLE EGYPT YOUTH SUMMIT 2025

The team president represented the organization at the SUSTAINABLE EGYPT YOUTH SUMMIT 2025, held at the American University in Cairo. The summit brought together young leaders from across the country to explore sustainable development initiatives, exchange innovative ideas, and engage in workshops focused on social impact, environmental responsibility, and youth empowerment. The participation highlighted the organization’s commitment to fostering leadership, promoting national engagement, and connecting students with broader societal initiatives.

November 2025 | Educational & Skill Development Achievement

Collaborative Student Training Workshop

The organization participated in organizing a student training camp in collaboration with external student families from Assiut University. The event was held in partnership with the student training entity “Al-Bawslagiya Team” and in cooperation with the British Council. The program included several training tracks focusing on effective study techniques and practical methods for using AI tools to support academic learning. The initiative aimed to enhance students’ academic performance, develop modern learning skills, and help students adapt to evolving educational technologies. The event was attended by approximately 300 students, and all participants received an accredited certificate upon completing the program.

November 2025 | Educational & Cultural Achievement

Visit to the Grand Egyptian Museum

The organization organized an educational visit to the newly opened Grand Egyptian Museum. The trip aimed to enhance members’ understanding of Egypt’s rich archaeological heritage, foster cultural appreciation, and provide a direct learning experience with significant historical artifacts. This initiative aligns with the organization’s mission to support experiential learning and strengthen cultural awareness among students.

November 2025 | Sports & Team-Building Achievement

Inter-Class Football Tournament

the organization held an inter-class football tournament within the college, featuring teams from Fourth years. The event aimed to foster healthy competition, teamwork, and student engagement, as part of the strategic plan to encourage rivalry with other college branches across various governorates. The winning team was officially recognized and will represent the college in competing against other branches. The tournament was conducted under the kind patronage of the Academy President, reflecting the organization’s commitment to promoting sportsmanship, collaboration, and campus-wide participation.

December 2025 | Educational & Cultural Achievement

Champion Mix Challenge – College Competition

The organization hosted the "Champion Mix Challenge" within the college, a competition designed to enhance students’ engagement in campus activities and foster teamwork, creativity, and critical thinking. The event combined cultural and educational elements, encouraging students to apply knowledge in a collaborative environment. The winning team was officially recognized and honored, highlighting the organization’s commitment to motivating student participation and celebrating achievement.

December 2025 | Volunteer Work & Community Development

Campus Tree & Planting Initiative

The team president, along with several members, participated in planting trees and various plants within the university campus. The initiative aimed to promote environmental responsibility, strengthen community engagement, and encourage students to contribute actively to the development and beautification of their campus. This activity reflects the organization’s commitment to volunteer work, community development, and fostering a culture of active participation among students.

December 2025 | Educational & Professional Development Achievement

Student Skills Development Seminar – Minya Branch

The organization conducted a specialized seminar aimed at intensifying and enhancing students’ academic and professional skills. The event was attended by a distinguished group of professors and teaching assistants. The seminar covered key topics including how students can keep pace with rapid developments, how to build integrated skill sets aligned with labor market demands, and a dedicated track on effectively using AI tools to support academic performance. This initiative reflects the organization’s commitment to equipping students with practical competencies, future-oriented thinking, and the tools necessary to succeed in a competitive academic and professional environment.

JANUARY 2026 | RECREATIONAL & TEAM-BUILDING ACTIVITY

Adventure Fun Day

An exciting recreational day organized for the family members, filled with fun, adventures, and unforgettable moments. The day brought everyone together through a variety of entertaining activities and challenges, creating a positive atmosphere, strengthening team spirit, and building stronger connections among the members.

FEBRUARY 2026 | RAMADAN ACTIVITIES & Bringing Our Campus to Life

Ramadan Activities

A collection of special Ramadan activities organized for the family members, bringing everyone together in a warm and joyful atmosphere. The activities included fun moments, meaningful gatherings, and team-based experiences that strengthened friendships, encouraged teamwork, and created unforgettable memories throughout the holy month.

MARCH 2026 | MEMBERS APPRECIATION

Celebrating Our Outstanding Members

A special event to celebrate our outstanding family members and thank them for their hard work and active participation. It was a great chance to appreciate their efforts, celebrate their achievements, and encourage everyone to keep growing and working together as a team.
